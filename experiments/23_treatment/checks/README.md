# Checks of treatment behaviour

Short runs, made before exp 23's main runs, that check how the treatment design ([CONVENTIONS.md, section 9](../../../CONVENTIONS.md#9-treatment)) and the existing models behave. Each script's output is in the `.log` of the same name.

**Versions:** the v4 checks use `v4/.venv` (tbsim `dcfb522`, starsim `80c81f53`); run them from the repo root with `PYTHONPATH=v4 v4/.venv/bin/python`. `v010_relapse.py` ran twice: in the repo root's `.venv` (tbsim `b92d7e8`, starsim 3.6.1), and in a temporary environment at the versions of tb_LAI_TPT's calibration `hm_real_20260728` (tbsim `85a977a`, starsim `8e7868a`, from its `_snapshot/metadata.json`).

| Script | Question | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `relapse_abm.py` | Is v4's relapse after a fixed 1.5 years, or at a rate? | **A rate.** A plain `dur=ss.years(1.5)` is the mean of an exponential, in both solvers: 0.368 of people still waiting at 1.5 years in the ODE, 0.378 as agents, against e^(−1) = 0.368. `ss.constant(...)` gives a fixed 1.5 years in both |
| `tx_design.py` | Does the proposed structure run in both solvers? Starting at a rate with the outcome drawn at entry, a fixed or exponential course, and a fixed exit that branches to `treated` or `relapsing` | **Yes,** in both solvers, with matching flows. The success share is s, and the relapse share of successes is `p_relapse` |
| `tx_timing.py` | Does a fixed course last exactly 6 months? Is a time-varying success probability applied at the start? Can agents fail and restart in the same step? | **Exactly 6 monthly steps,** in agents and the ODE. **Applied at the start:** exits in early 2001 succeed at the 2000 value, 0.20 (agents 0.206). **Same-step restarts happen,** for 39% of failures at θ = 12 per year: 1 − e^(−θ·dt/2), as in continuous time when failures fall within the step. That isn't the v0.10 pitfall of re-initiation through stale flags. At θ = 1.1 per year it's about 5% of failures |
| `ts_lag.py` | Does a change in an `ss.timeseries` take effect without a lag? | **Yes,** at the first step that starts on or after the change. Results at index i hold the transitions of the step ending at i (index 0 is the starting state). Monthly steps (30.44 days) don't line up with calendar months, so a change dated on the 1st of a month can fall one step later than expected |
| `fail_death.py` | Can people whose treatment will fail face TB death at `sym_dead` while on a fixed or exponential 6-month course? | **Yes, in both solvers.** For people starting treatment during the run, the share of courses ending in TB death is within 0.7% of exact at monthly and weekly steps: 18.0% for a fixed course, 16.5% for a rate. **Starting people already on a fixed course** in the ODE adds about half a step of exposure (19.3% at monthly steps), so a start at a treated equilibrium needs the time already on treatment set |
| `v010_relapse.py` | v0.10 `Tx` as tb_LAI_TPT runs it: how long is the course, and how long to relapse? | **Course:** 5.92 steps, completing at the 6th, as intended (tb_LAI_TPT passes the number 180, read as days). **Relapse: 0.59 steps after treatment ends, not 1.5 years,** at both sets of versions. The default `dur_relapse = ss.constant(v=ss.years(1.5))` is converted to the sim's unit (18 months), and `Tx` then divides it by the days per step as if it were days. So a relapse fires at the next step, about 1 month after cure |
